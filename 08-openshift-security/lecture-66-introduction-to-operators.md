# Lecture 66: Introduction to Operators

**Operators** are a method of packaging, deploying, and managing a Kubernetes application.

### The Operator Concept
An Operator is like a "packaged human expert" in software form. It watches your application and takes action to ensure it stays healthy.
- **Install:** Handles complex setup.
- **Update:** Manages seamless version upgrades.
- **Heal:** Automatically fixes the app if it drifts from the desired state.

OpenShift itself is built entirely of Operators (the "Console Operator," the "Network Operator," etc.).
