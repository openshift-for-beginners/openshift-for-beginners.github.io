# Lecture 72: Bonus - Next Steps

Congratulations on completing the fundamentals!

### Where to go from here?
1. **Certification:** Look into the **Red Hat Certified Enterprise Application Developer** or **Administrator** exams.
2. **Advanced Topics:**
    - **Service Mesh (Istio):** Advanced traffic management and security.
    - **Serverless (Knative):** Event-driven scaling.
    - **Advanced Cluster Management (ACM):** Managing multiple clusters.
3. **Practice:** Continue using the **Red Hat Developer Sandbox** to experiment with complex YAML manifests.
